About MeTentang Saya

My background is in computer networking. I studied Computer and Network Engineering at SMK Negeri 9 Medan, where I set up DNS, web, mail and FTP servers on Debian and connected them into a local network that works without internet access.

Along the way I became interested in cyber security, and that interest led me to programming: to understand how a system is attacked and defended, I wanted to understand how it is built. Today I build web and mobile applications while continuing to study networks and operating systems.
